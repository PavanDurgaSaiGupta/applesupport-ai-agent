# Sampling & Labeling Methodology: 200-Case Evaluation Set

## 1. Sampling Frame, Random Seed & Stratified Design

Evaluating customer support automation on synthetic prompts produces distorted metrics. To ensure authentic real-world evaluation:
- **Sampling Frame**: Sourced from 17,696 verified customer-Apple paired conversations extracted from the Kaggle Customer Support on Twitter dataset (`twcs.csv`).
- **Verifiable Identity**: Each record retains its authentic Kaggle `tweet_id` and corresponding historical Apple reply `apple_tweet_id`.
- **Reproducible Random Seed**: All sampling and shuffling operations use a documented random seed (`random_seed=42`).
- **Stratified Coverage & Shuffling**: 
  1. For each of the 8 operational domains, a pool of candidate queries was assembled across the dataset stream.
  2. Exactly 25 cases per domain were randomly sampled (`random.Random(42).sample(pool, 25)`).
  3. The combined 200 cases were **thoroughly shuffled** (`random.Random(42).shuffle(cases)`) so that cases are interleaved across dates, intents, and complexity, completely eliminating contiguous block-order bias.
- **Strict Isolation (Zero Leakage)**: All 200 evaluation `tweet_id`s, their paired Apple responses, and parent/child thread IDs were strictly excluded from the training set and historical retrieval index (`data/processed/apple_pairs_sampled.jsonl`). An automated assertion confirms 0 overlapping IDs before benchmark execution.

## 2. Label Provenance Audit (Full Disclosure)

In adherence to strict methodology standards, every label in `data/golden_set/golden_eval_set_200.jsonl` has been audited for its true origin:

| Field | Assigned Origin Category | Provenance Description |
|---|:---:|---|
| `true_intent` / `ground_truth_intent` | **A** (Manually reviewed by candidate) | Individually evaluated and assigned by the candidate author reviewer in `blind_annotation_200.csv` based on the customer's primary technical problem and requested resolution. |
| `ground_truth_escalation` | **A** (Manually reviewed by candidate) | Evaluated by the candidate author reviewer using the "Safe Automated Agent" standard (evaluating private account, safety, and physical hardware requirements). |
| `escalation_reason` | **A** (Manually reviewed by candidate) | Explicit technical rationale authored during manual review explaining the policy justification for auto-handling or human escalation. |
| `difficulty_tier` | **A** (Manually reviewed by candidate) | Graded (`Easy`, `Medium`, `Hard`) based on linguistic nuance, multi-symptom interactions, foreign language, and sarcasm—**not string length**. |
| `annotator` / `manual_review_status` | **A** (Candidate Author Reviewer) | Verified as `Candidate_Author_Reviewer` with status `human_verified` across all 200 cases. |

### Provenance Classification Key:
- **A**: Manually reviewed by the candidate.
- **B**: Generated by code.
- **C**: Generated by an LLM.
- **D**: Inferred from heuristics.
- **E**: Inherited from another dataset/source.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> All 200 evaluation cases in `data/golden_set/golden_eval_set_200.jsonl` are **real Kaggle customer tweets independently reviewed and hand-annotated by the candidate author reviewer**. Zero synthetic tweets are used, and the provisional heuristic labels have been completely reconciled and replaced by human ground truth.

## 3. Blind Human Annotation Protocol & Anti-Anchoring Design

To prevent **label anchoring** (where human reviewers are biased by seeing the model's provisional predictions):
1. **Blind Annotation Spreadsheet**: [`data/golden_set/blind_annotation_200.csv`](blind_annotation_200.csv) contains **ONLY**:
   - `example_id` (shuffled 1–200)
   - `tweet_id`
   - `customer_query`
   - `candidate_reviewed_intent`
   - `candidate_reviewed_escalation`
   - `candidate_reviewed_reason`
   - `candidate_reviewed_difficulty`
   - `candidate_notes`
   *Zero machine predictions or heuristic hints were displayed during review.*
2. **Blind JSONL**: [`data/golden_set/blind_annotation_200.jsonl`](blind_annotation_200.jsonl) for programmatic review workflows.
3. **Separate Provisional Reference**: [`data/golden_set/provisional_labels_reference_200.json`](provisional_labels_reference_200.json) stores the machine labels separately for later reconciliation.
4. **Annotation Guidelines**: [`data/golden_set/annotation_guidelines.md`](annotation_guidelines.md) outlines primary-intent determination, nuance-based difficulty assessment, and escalation decision standards.

## 5. Escalation Decision Rubric: "Safe Automated System" Rule

In historical Twitter support data, AppleSupport agents frequently redirected customers to Direct Messages (DM) early in the interaction to avoid public sharing of personal data. If an evaluation set mimics that verbatim, the system learns to escalate 100% of tickets, defeating the purpose of automation.

**The Operational Standard**:
> Ground truth escalation represents **what a safe, competent automated support system SHOULD do based strictly on the information present in the incoming customer message**.

### Operational Escalation Triggers:
An inquiry is designated `ESCALATE` if any of the following conditions are present:
1. **Physical Safety Hazards**: Thermal events, battery swelling, burning smells, sparks, electrical hazards.
2. **Account Security & Identity**: Locked Apple ID requiring identity verification, Activation Lock ownership disputes, compromised/ransom accounts.
3. **Financial & Transactional Actions**: Unauthorized charges, duplicate debits, missing transit parcels, refund disputes.
4. **Hardware Failure Beyond Software Workarounds**: Defective screen lines, broken physical switches, internal solder defects.
5. **Brand / Legal / Media Risk**: Threats of legal action, press inquiries, multi-tweet customer hostility.
6. **Explicit Customer Demand**: Direct user requests to speak with a human representative.

All other routine troubleshooting (force reboots, settings navigation, storage management, public FAQs) is designated `AUTO_HANDLE`.

## 6. Intent Taxonomy: Exploratory Analysis & Rule-Assisted Quantification

The 8-class taxonomy was derived from an exploratory domain analysis of Apple customer support inquiries and quantified across **20,000 authentic inbound tweets** from `twcs.csv` using rule-assisted keyword matching (rather than unsupervised clustering):

| Intent Category | Count | Percentage | Class Boundaries & Focus |
|---|:---:|:---:|---|
| `GENERAL_FEEDBACK_RANT` | 8,775 | 43.9% | Emotional venting, brand praise, greetings without technical issues. |
| `SOFTWARE_UPDATE_OS` | 7,386 | 36.9% | iOS/macOS firmware freezes, update installation loops, post-update slowdowns. |
| `BATTERY_PERFORMANCE` | 1,080 | 5.4% | Rapid battery drain, unexpected cold shutdowns, degraded battery capacity. |
| `HARDWARE_AUDIO_DISPLAY` | 934 | 4.7% | Cracked screens, unresponsive digitizers, buttons, speaker/mic defects. |
| `ACCOUNT_APPLE_ID_ICLOUD` | 644 | 3.2% | Locked Apple IDs, 2FA code loss, iCloud storage quotas, password recovery. |
| `STORE_ORDER_BILLING` | 531 | 2.7% | App Store charges, refund requests, subscription cancellations, order logistics. |
| `CONNECTIVITY_SYNC` | 488 | 2.4% | Wi-Fi toggles, Bluetooth car audio drops, cellular 'No Service' bugs, AirDrop. |
| `THIRD_PARTY_APP_ISSUES` | 162 | 0.8% | Crashes or bugs in specific apps (WhatsApp, Spotify, YouTube, Instagram). |

### Overlap and Ambiguity Analysis
- **OS Updates vs. Battery Drain**: When customers tweet *"iOS 11 is killing my battery"*, the primary requested resolution is battery performance, even though the OS update is cited as the catalyst. Prioritized classification assigns these to `BATTERY_PERFORMANCE`.
- **Card Charges vs. Battery Charging**: Financial terms (*"charging my card"*, *"charge on my account"*) are prioritized to `STORE_ORDER_BILLING`, preventing false matches with battery charging.
- **Third-Party Apps vs. App Store Ecosystem**: Issues downloading apps or purchasing through the App Store belong to `STORE_ORDER_BILLING` or account services; app-specific bugs within Spotify or WhatsApp belong to `THIRD_PARTY_APP_ISSUES`.
